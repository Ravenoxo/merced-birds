Merced Winter Birds

Frequencies come from eBird records for Merced County in November–December. They cover the whole county, not just the refuges. Places and ID tips are guidance only; birds move, so go by what you see in the field and by recent eBird reports.

Photo links need a connection. Signal on the refuges is patchy, so open the ones you want ahead of time. Your “seen” marks are saved only in this browser on this phone.
